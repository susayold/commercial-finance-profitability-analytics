# CFO memo v1 — VietNova Consumer JSC

## Executive readout

The v1 model is a controlled synthetic case for a consumer business. FY2025 revenue is VND 69.2bn, EBITDA proxy is VND 17.3bn and the dashboard shows a 24.9% EBITDA proxy margin. The model status is PASS after six automated controls.

These figures are synthetic management outputs, not company-reported facts. They are designed to demonstrate the analyst workflow and should be labelled accordingly in any portfolio presentation.

## What changed

- Revenue grows through the 36-month horizon with recurring seasonal spikes around promotional months.
- The budget is scenario-driven, so the analyst can test Base, Upside and Downside assumptions without rewriting actuals.
- Contribution margin is calculated after COGS, channel fees and trade spend; this prevents a revenue-only view from hiding weak channel economics.
- CCC is calculated from DSO, DIO and DPO and is surfaced beside profitability to connect earnings to cash.

## Recommended management actions

1. Protect contribution margin in channels below the 25% hurdle: require a promotion ROI check before incremental trade spend.
2. Use the channel table as the weekly commercial-finance agenda: revenue gap, fee leakage, trade-spend efficiency and mix.
3. Set a cash-release target from DSO/DIO/DPO movement and assign owners in the working-capital review.
4. Replace proxy OCF and Opex lines with chart-of-accounts mappings before treating the workbook as a production forecast.

## Risks and caveats

- The company, transactions, customers and promotions are fictional.
- Public peer filings are used for calibration and benchmarking only.
- OCR-derived peer numbers remain in a human-review queue; none are treated as approved facts.
- The workbook is an analyst portfolio artifact, not an investment recommendation or statutory forecast.

## Decision log
