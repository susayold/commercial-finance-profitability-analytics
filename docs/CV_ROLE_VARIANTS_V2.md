# Role-targeted CV Variants V2

Use the one-page CV V3 as the base. Swap only the headline, profile and first two project bullets to match the vacancy. Keep the evidence class language; do not imply employment, private company access, realised impact or a live market recommendation.

## Variant A — Junior FP&A / Finance Analyst (primary)

**Headline:** Finance Analyst | FP&A | Budgeting, Forecasting & Performance Management

**Profile:** Finance analyst focused on management P&L, annual budgeting, rolling forecast, variance/PVM and working-capital control. Strong at tracing a financial gap to operational drivers, reconciling the answer and assigning a practical owner/action.

**Lead bullets:**

- Built a 28-tab, 36-month FP&A model connecting actuals, FY2025 budget, forecast versions, PVM, profitability, working capital, liquidity and scenarios; nine visible controls passed (**SIMULATED operating case**).
- Designed a leakage-safe forecast workflow and CFO operating pack with freeze rules, variance bridges, cash triggers, KPI dictionary, recommendation register and WD-5–WD+5 cadence (**SIMULATED / DERIVED**).

**Keywords:** FP&A, budget, forecast, P&L, variance, PVM, management reporting, working capital, CCC, OPEX, CAPEX, Excel, controls.

## Variant B — Business Finance / Commercial Finance Analyst

**Headline:** Commercial Finance Analyst | Profitability, Pricing & Business Partnering

**Profile:** Commercial finance analyst focused on gross-to-net, customer/channel contribution, pricing, promotion ROI and trade-spend decisions. Connects revenue growth to value, cash and guardrails instead of reporting topline alone.

**Lead bullets:**

- Mapped discounts, rebates, platform fees, trade spend and cost-to-serve by channel/customer; evaluated promotion and pricing cases against a 25% contribution hurdle (**SIMULATED / DERIVED**).
- Built MBR, battle-card and recommendation-register outputs that translate a P&L driver into value equation, owner, stop/scale rule and next review date (**SIMULATED / DERIVED**).

**Keywords:** commercial finance, business partnering, gross-to-net, pricing, trade spend, A&P, promotion ROI, customer/channel profitability, decision support.

## Variant C — Finance Data Analyst (secondary)

**Headline:** Finance Data Analyst | FP&A Analytics | Reconciliation & Semantic Modelling

**Profile:** Finance-first analyst who designs traceable data structures for planning and performance reporting. Builds semantic contracts, calculations and QA so dashboards support decisions on revenue, margin, cash and forecast risk.

**Lead bullets:**

- Authored a Power BI-ready contract covering 5 dimensions, 11 fact tables, 17 relationships, six report pages and QA definitions; native `.pbix` remains a documented Desktop gate.
- Wired deterministic validators for completeness, tie-outs, leakage, scorecards and public-finance reports into a repeatable GitHub Actions QA workflow (**CALCULATED / SIMULATED**).

**Keywords:** finance data, semantic model, reconciliation, Power BI, DAX, data quality, automation, GitHub Actions, finance metrics.

## Variant D — Fundamental / Equity Research Analyst (supporting)

**Headline:** Fundamental Analyst | Financial Statement Analysis | Valuation Rehearsal

**Profile:** Finance analyst with a public-filing research lens: decomposes growth, margin, cash conversion and capital signals into a thesis, catalyst/risk map and diligence agenda. Uses EV-only valuation framing when equity-value inputs are incomplete.

**Lead bullets:**

- Analysed MCH FY2016–FY2025 public statements: 9.24% revenue CAGR, 10.34% PAT CAGR, FY2025 operating margin of 25.41% and CFO/PAT of 31.52%; kept the FY2017 comparative-column caveat visible (**PUBLIC / CALCULATED**).
- Produced a 1–5 fundamental scorecard (16/25), credit memo and Base/Upside/Downside FCFF/DCF frame; output stops at EV and publishes no price target without net debt, shares, market price and approved forecasts (**CALCULATED / ANALYST_ASSUMPTION_REHEARSAL**).

**Keywords:** financial statement analysis, earnings quality, cash conversion, ROA/ROE, DCF, enterprise value, catalysts, risks, diligence, investment thesis.

## Selection rules

| Vacancy language | Use first | De-emphasise |
|---|---|---|
| Budget, forecast, month-end, variance, CFO | Variant A | M&A and market-language detail |
| Sales, marketing, trade spend, pricing, ROI | Variant B | Long data-model explanation |
| Power BI, data model, dashboard, automation | Variant C | Unsupported SQL or live-accuracy claims |
| Equity, research, earnings, valuation, thesis | Variant D | Synthetic operating details before public evidence |

## Integrity line

> Synthetic operating ledger; public filings and public guidance used for calibration/benchmarking; valuation forecasts are analyst assumptions. Real internal forecast accuracy, native PBIX evidence and realised company impact remain pending external gates.
