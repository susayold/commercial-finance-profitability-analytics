# Assumptions and Limitations

## Synthetic operating data

VietNova's transaction, customer, SKU, promotion, inventory, AR, AP, budget and forecast tables are deterministic synthetic data. They are designed to simulate realistic commercial-finance decisions and are not evidence of actual VietNova performance.

## Public peer data

VNM, QNS and KDC figures are extracted from official annual reports or audited statements and retained with source URLs, page anchors, reporting basis and comparability notes. They benchmark scale and economics; they do not validate private VietNova operating facts.

## Comparability

- Peer revenue bases can differ between net revenue and total revenue.
- Consolidation perimeters and portfolio composition can change across years.
- FY2006 VNM is a restated comparative under VAS 25.
- FY2007 VNM PAT exceeds PBT because of reported tax / minority-interest presentation; this is documented, not treated as a formula error.
- Long-run CAGR is restricted when definitions or perimeters are not comparable.

## Allocations

Shared commercial OPEX is allocated using documented driver rules. Allocated operating profit is directional and should not be interpreted as a legal-entity segment disclosure.

## Promotion and scenario economics

Promotion uplift, cannibalization, price elasticity and scenario impacts are modeled assumptions. They are not causal estimates unless a valid experiment or counterfactual is added.

## Missing real-world controls

The case does not include ERP close governance, tax review, audit sign-off, customer contract legal terms or a production planning system. A real implementation would require Finance, Commercial, Supply Chain and Treasury owners to validate inputs.

## Publication rule

Do not publish raw private reports or imply company impact. Keep official reports in the private Drive archive and publish only source-linked extracts and clearly labelled synthetic operating data.
