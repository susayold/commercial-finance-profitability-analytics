# Business Case — VietNova Consumer JSC

## Role simulation

This portfolio simulates a Junior FP&A / Commercial Finance Analyst supporting the CFO and Commercial Director of a Vietnam omnichannel FMCG company. The analyst owns monthly management reporting, budget versus actual analysis, rolling forecast, product/customer/channel profitability and quantified decision support.

## Company profile

VietNova Consumer JSC is fictional. It sells packaged beverages and healthy snacks through General Trade, Modern Trade, E-commerce Marketplace, Direct-to-Consumer and Wholesale/Distributor channels across six Vietnamese regions. The operating ledger is deterministic synthetic data created for this case; no private company data is implied.

| Dimension | Scope |

## Management question

How can VietNova grow revenue while protecting contribution profit, operating profit and cash quality?

The model is intentionally designed to force trade-offs. Marketplace growth carries platform, voucher and fulfilment costs; Modern Trade carries rebates and longer payment terms; premium mix improves margin; slow-moving SKUs consume cash; and a high-revenue account can be unattractive after cost-to-serve.

## Embedded events to recover

1. Input-cost inflation raises unit COGS in a defined quarter.
2. Marketplace volume grows faster than contribution profit.
3. One large promotion creates volume but weak incremental contribution.
4. One key account has high revenue but excessive rebate and payment-term cost.
5. Mix shifts toward mass-market SKUs before premium mix recovers.
6. Selected SKUs accumulate slow-moving inventory.

## Required decision outputs

- Actual versus budget, prior year and latest forecast.
- Price–volume–mix and margin bridge with an explained residual.
- Product, customer and channel contribution views.
- Rolling forecast with version control, Bias and WAPE.
- Base, growth, margin-pressure and downside cases.
- Three to five quantified recommendations with owners, assumptions, risks and monitoring KPIs.

## Evidence boundary

Synthetic operating facts are labelled SIMULATED. Public peer data is OBSERVED and source-linked, while ratios and bridges are DERIVED. Forecast and scenario drivers are ASSUMPTIONS. Recommendations must never be presented as historical impact at a real company.
