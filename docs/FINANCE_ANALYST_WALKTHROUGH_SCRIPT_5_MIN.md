# Finance Analyst Walkthrough Script — 5 Minutes

**Release:** 2026-09-01 non-Power-BI FP&A package
**Audience:** CFO, FP&A hiring manager, commercial finance lead
**Purpose:** narrate the management pack as a decision story; this script is ready for a manual screen recording.

> Evidence boundary: VietNova operating data is deterministic synthetic data. The walkthrough demonstrates finance method, controls and decision logic; it does not claim employer impact or live forecast accuracy.

## 00:00–00:30 — Open with the decision

“This is a monthly operating review for VietNova Consumer. The question is not whether revenue can grow; it is whether growth converts into contribution and cash. My recommendation is to approve the base plan with explicit cash gates.”

Point to the base revenue, EBITDA proxy and CCC KPIs. Explain that all figures are in VND bn unless noted and that the evidence boundary is visible on every slide.

## 00:30–01:00 — Frame the scorecard

“The base scenario shows VND 82.5bn of revenue, VND 26.9bn of gross profit and a 15.6% EBITDA proxy margin. The downside case compresses the margin to 4.5%, so the operating conversation must be about drivers, not just the top line.”

State the management rule: keep the base plan while margin is at least 15% and CCC is no more than 58 days; escalate after two consecutive breaches.

## 01:00–01:35 — Walk the P&L bridge

“The bridge makes the economics auditable: revenue less COGS creates gross profit, then controllable OPEX determines the EBITDA proxy. I use this view to ask three questions: which channel is buying growth with discounts, which SKU has cost leakage, and which OPEX line can be flexed this month?”

Clarify that EBITDA is a proxy, not statutory EBITDA. This is an intentional management measure with a published definition.

## 01:35–02:10 — Explain commercial profitability

“The commercial lens separates volume, price, mix, discounts, trade spend and COGS/GM. That prevents a false ‘growth is good’ conclusion. The action map protects high-contribution growth, fixes low-margin revenue, reprices leakage and exits negative after-working-capital accounts.”

Connect the register to an owner: Commercial owns discount approvals; Supply Chain owns inventory ageing; Finance and AR own collection.

## 02:10–02:50 — Make cash the constraint

“The integrated model links P&L to working capital, cash flow and the balance sheet. DSO and DIO are high, while supplier terms partially fund the cycle. The FY2025 calendar model produces negative CFO and negative closing cash, so I would not release discretionary growth capex until CFO is positive and the minimum cash buffer is restored.”

Call out that the negative cash outcome is a deliberate stress signal for the case, not a live company forecast.

## 02:50–03:25 — Show forecast discipline

“Forecast accuracy is versioned, not anecdotal. Budget, RF1, RF2, RF3 and Latest Estimate are frozen at a cutoff and measured with Bias, WAPE and MAE. The synthetic rehearsal shows the expected improvement as the horizon shortens, but live accuracy is still Gate A: we need an approved internal pre-close snapshot and post-close actuals.”

Point to the version bridge and override log as the audit trail.

## 03:25–04:00 — Explain the three-year plan

“The operating plan uses monthly rows for FY2026 review cadence and quarterly rows for FY2027–FY2028. Each scenario is driver-based and carries initiative owners, investment, benefit, risk, stage gate and kill criteria. There is no terminal growth assumption.”

Emphasise that the plan is a planning rehearsal and should not be presented as approved company guidance.

## 04:00–04:30 — Close the costing loop

“Standard costing turns a gross-margin miss into an operating action. The bridge separates purchase price, usage/yield and conversion overhead, while inventory reserve is tracked separately. Procurement should lock supplier assumptions at freeze; Operations should investigate yield loss by SKU and plant.”

Use the control language: bridge residual must be zero; reserve movement requires an explicit owner and review date.

## 04:30–05:00 — Close with actions and next gate

“Every insight ends with an owner, a date and a guardrail. The five immediate actions are cash conversion, inventory ageing, discount leakage, cost variance and forecast cadence. The next release decision is Gate A: load the approved internal forecast and post-close actuals, then replace the rehearsal accuracy with live Bias/WAPE.”

Finish with: “The value of FP&A is making this decision repeatable next month, with the same definitions, evidence and controls.”
