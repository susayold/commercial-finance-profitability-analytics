# Role-targeted CV variants

Use one base CV and swap the summary plus the first two project bullets for the vacancy. Keep the same evidence boundary and never imply paid employment, private company data or a native PBIX before the relevant gate closes.

## Variant A — Junior FP&A / Finance Analyst

**Headline:** Finance Analyst | FP&A | Budgeting, Forecasting & Performance Management

**Summary:** Finance analyst building driver-based planning, rolling forecast, P&L, variance and working-capital models. Strong at turning assumptions into an auditable management pack, explaining price/volume/mix drivers and recommending actions with explicit risks and controls.

**Project bullets (priority order):**

- Built a 36-month driver-based FP&A model with budget and forecast versions, formula-driven P&L, working-capital schedules and nine control checks.
- Produced an actual-vs-plan variance bridge using price, volume and mix drivers; translated the largest gaps into owner, timing and mitigation actions in a CFO memo.
- Added forecast-freeze governance, leakage tests and a reusable Bias/WAPE workflow; synthetic and public-guidance evidence are clearly separated from the pending real internal snapshot.

**Keywords:** FP&A, annual budget, rolling forecast, management reporting, variance analysis, P&L, working capital, cash conversion, scenario analysis, Excel, financial modelling, controls.

## Variant B — Business Finance / Commercial Finance Analyst

**Headline:** Commercial Finance Analyst | Profitability, Pricing & Business Partnering

**Summary:** Commercial finance analyst focused on gross-to-net performance, customer/channel profitability, pricing and promotion ROI. Combines financial modelling with a business-partner narrative that connects volume to value, identifies risks and assigns practical actions to Sales and Marketing stakeholders.

**Project bullets (priority order):**

- Mapped revenue, trade spend, A&P and contribution margin by channel, customer and product to expose the commercial drivers behind the P&L gap.
- Built promotion/pricing and D2C unit-economics cases with pre/post ROI logic, sensitivity ranges and explicit break-even thresholds.
- Prepared a management-ready decision pack that links commercial assumptions to forecast, cash and profitability implications rather than reporting isolated KPIs.

**Keywords:** commercial finance, business partnering, gross-to-net, pricing, trade terms, A&P, promotion ROI, P&L simulation, profitability, volume-to-value, management reporting, Power BI.

## Variant C — Finance Data Analyst (secondary)

**Headline:** Finance Data Analyst | FP&A Analytics | Power BI-Ready Semantic Modelling

**Summary:** Finance analyst who designs traceable data structures for planning and performance reporting. Builds finance-first semantic contracts, measures and reconciliation checks so dashboards support decisions on revenue, margin, cash and forecast risk.

**Project bullets (priority order):**

- Designed a finance star-schema contract covering dimensions, facts, relationships, measures, report pages and QA definitions for a Power BI implementation.
- Added automated completeness, reconciliation, leakage and forecast-accuracy validators; wired the checks into GitHub Actions for repeatable review.
- Documented PBIP handoff, refresh ownership and QA-01–QA-18 execution steps; native `.pbix` completion remains an explicit Gate B item.

**Keywords:** finance data, semantic model, star schema, Power BI, DAX, data quality, reconciliation, SQL (only if separately demonstrated), GitHub Actions, reproducible analysis.

## Evidence add-ons (use only when the vacancy rewards operating-pack depth)

Keep the core project bullets concise, then select one proof point when the application or interview rewards operating-finance depth:

- **Monthly Business Review / CFO pack:** reference the management-pack workflow (KPI dictionary, recommendation register, close/forecast cadence and risk log) with 10/10 QA; describe it as an operating-pack design built on synthetic/public-guidance evidence, not as an employer deliverable.
- **OPEX/CAPEX and cash discipline:** surface the OPEX/CAPEX taxonomy, budget-owner controls, cash bridge and working-capital schedule when the role stresses cost control, investment governance or liquidity.
- **FY2017 indexed evidence:** use the eight-metric MCH FY2017 web-index layer only as a controlled public-data appendix (8/8 QA, `INDEXED_ONLY`); do not blend it into the approved provenance set or imply archived source bytes.
- **Interview bridge:** explain how the same evidence hierarchy would be promoted to `LIVE_INTERNAL` only after receipt of a real pre-close forecast snapshot with owner, cutoff, approval, actual-availability and period fields.

This creates a visibly deeper finance-operator narrative while preserving the same claim boundary across all three variants.
