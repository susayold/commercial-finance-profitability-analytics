# Known Limitations — 2026-09-01

1. VietNova operating data is deterministic synthetic data; it cannot support claims of employer impact.
2. EBITDA is an explicit proxy (`gross profit − controllable OPEX`), not reported statutory EBITDA.
3. Monte Carlo outputs are seeded scenario distributions, not probabilistic estimates calibrated to live history; the v2 matrix is judgmental.
4. M&A outputs are a synthetic screening rehearsal; confirmatory diligence, financing and tax review are absent.
5. Automated commentary is a first draft and remains NEEDS_REVIEW until a human approves it.
6. SAP-like mapping is a simulated FI/CO rehearsal and does not evidence SAP production experience.
7. Gate A remains an external evidence gate as documented above.
